When your company turns on face photos, you take a quick selfie to clock out, and to clock in if your company asks for it. An outline on the camera helps you line up your face, and the info button explains what the photo is for.

When your company assigns an end-of-shift form, you answer it when you clock out.

When your company turns on location logging, the app records your location during your shift, including breaks, and stops at clock-out.
